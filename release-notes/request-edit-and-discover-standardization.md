# Request edit and Discover standardization

- Refreshes Movie, Series, Music, and Book edit-request dialogs as centered
  artwork-backed cards with darker inset content and standard actions.
- Applies the site-background card and inset-message treatment to Delete Request
  and Delete From Library confirmations.
- Standardizes Discover title visibility, refresh, previous, and next controls;
  Refresh is orange while previous and next are green.
- Removes the legacy white selected-value surface from Discover filter dropdowns.
- Uses original-resolution TMDB color logos for Studio and Network buttons and
  their linked pages, which now reuse the complete Movie or Series filters and
  retain a named top-left heading.
